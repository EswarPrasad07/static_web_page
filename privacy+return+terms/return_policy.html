Return Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

At FreshCart, customer satisfaction is our priority. We strive to deliver fresh and high-quality fruits. If you are not satisfied with your purchase, please review our return policy below.

1. Eligible Returns

You may request a return or replacement if:

  • The fruits are damaged during delivery.
  • You receive the wrong items.
  • The products are spoiled or not fresh upon delivery.

2. Return Request

Return requests must be made within 24 hours of receiving your order. Please provide your order details along with clear photos of the product.

3. Non-Returnable Items

The following items are not eligible for return:

  • Products consumed or partially consumed.
  • Products damaged after delivery due to improper storage.
  • Return requests made after 24 hours of delivery.

4. Refund Policy

  • Approved refunds will be processed within 5–7 business days.
  • Refunds will be credited to the original payment method.
  • In some cases, a replacement product may be offered instead of a refund.

5. Order Cancellation

  • Orders can be cancelled before they are dispatched.
  • Once an order has been shipped, it cannot be cancelled.
